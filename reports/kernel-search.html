MOSSE kernel search

One kernel per width and height. MOSSE training uses σ = 2 px and λ = 0.01. The cosine window is multiplied into the kernel once after training. Detection is normalized cross-correlation over the bore. Each kernel is fit on one sweep plus the steady clips and scored on the other sweep, then the sweeps swap. Bins are the share of held-out sweep frames whose vertical miss is in that range, in native pixels on the 2592×1944 frame. Mac milliseconds are one OpenCV thread. Source: the labeled clips recorded on 22 Oct 2026.

Misses above 20 px

Each cell is one kernel. The number is the percent of held-out frames more than 20 px off. Width and height are native pixels, from 1× to 3× the 88 px ball. Darker is worse.
